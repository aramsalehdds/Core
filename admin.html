🔥 Ready to upgrade to Firebase (Cloud Sync)?
Currently all data is stored in the browser. This works great on one device for testing. To sync approvals across devices and make it permanent, connect Firebase (100% free for your scale).

When ready, ask Claude to:
  • Create your Firebase project at console.firebase.google.com
  • Integrate Firebase Auth — students get real accounts with email verification
  • Sync the approved users list to Firebase Realtime Database
  • Add automatic email notifications when a student registers
  • Build a CliQ reference number field in the registration form
This upgrade takes ~30 minutes and nothing changes visually for your students.
