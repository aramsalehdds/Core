05
Brand Voice
Core speaks like a brilliant clinical mentor — confident, precise, and never condescending. We make complexity feel approachable without watering it down.
🎯
Precise
Every word earns its place. We say exactly what needs to be said, nothing more.
🔬
Clinical
We use proper clinical language. Students should leave us sounding like doctors.
🤝
Accessible
Complex concepts, clear explanations. Depth without intimidation.
💎
Premium
Every guide is thorough and complete. Never superficial.
TONE
Confident, not arrogant. Friendly, not casual.
✓ DO:"The lateral border of the tongue is the most common site for oral SCC in the developed world."
✗ DON'T:"Hey guys!! Did you know tongue cancer is super common?? Check this out 🤩🤩"
✗ DON'T:"This guide comprehensively elucidates the multifaceted aetiopathogenesis of oral squamous cell carcinoma in a scholarly manner."
CAPTIONS / SOCIAL
Short. Sharp. Clinical.
✓ DO:"Non-healing ulcer for 3 weeks. Normal exam findings. What's your next step? — Answer in our oral cancer guide. 🔗 core.dental"
✗ DON'T:"Omgggg our new guide just dropped!! Study with us bestie 💅✨ Link in bio!!"
GUIDE WRITING STYLE
Clinical precision. No filler. No fluff.
✓ DO:"Erythroplakia: a fiery red velvety patch that cannot be attributed to another cause. The most dangerous PMD — 40–50% are already malignant at first biopsy."
✗ DON'T:"In this next section we will be talking about erythroplakia which is a very important topic that every dental student should know about and we will cover it in detail."
